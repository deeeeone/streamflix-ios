Direct IPA download:

```

https://github.com/deeeeone/streamflix-ios/releases/latest/download/StreamFlix.ipa

```

> **Requirements:** iOS 17 or higher. Free Apple IDs work fine (apps are re-signed locally).

---

### Sideloading Guides

Choose your preferred signing method below:

#### Method 1: AltStore (Recommended)

1. **Install AltServer** on your computer from [altstore.io](https://altstore.io/).
2. Connect your iPhone to your computer via USB (enable "Sync over Wi-Fi" for convenience).
3. Open **AltServer**, click its icon in the system tray / menu bar, and select **Install AltStore** > choose your device.
4. On your iOS device:
   - Go to **Settings > General > VPN & Device Management** and trust your Apple ID certificate.
   - Open **AltStore**, navigate to the **My Apps** tab, and tap the **`+`** icon at the top left.
5. Select the downloaded `StreamFlix.ipa` to install.

---

#### Method 2: Sideloadly

1. **Install Sideloadly** on your computer from [sideloadly.io](https://sideloadly.io/).
2. Connect your iPhone to your computer via USB and open **Sideloadly**.
3. Enter your **Apple ID** email in the designated field.
4. Drag and drop the downloaded `StreamFlix.ipa` into the Sideloadly window.
5. Click **Start** and enter your Apple ID password when prompted.
6. Once completed, go to **Settings > General > VPN & Device Management** on your iPhone and trust your Apple ID certificate.
